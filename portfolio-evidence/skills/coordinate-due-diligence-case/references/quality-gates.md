# Quality gates

Before human review, confirm:

- every required routine is present, blocked with a reason, or not applicable;
- every material claim is verified, contradicted, or outstanding;
- every serious concern appears in the flag register;
- every flag cites an actual source and evidence;
- every condition maps to a finding and has an owner and deadline or cadence;
- the overall risk narrative matches the most serious unresolved finding;
- missing or single-source evidence is not described as clean;
- source provenance reaches the underlying register, document, interview, or provider output where available;
- the final output retains dissent, contradictions, and data gaps;
- the final accountable decision is assigned to a human reviewer.
