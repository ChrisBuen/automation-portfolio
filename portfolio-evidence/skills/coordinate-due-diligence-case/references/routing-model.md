# Routing model

Route by the subject and the claims that require verification, not by folder name alone.

| Case feature | Typical bounded routine |
|---|---|
| Natural person | identity/career research; formal background checks |
| Company | official entity research; ownership/controllers; principal research |
| Fund or product | entity/principal research; mandate and market; counsel; infrastructure/service providers |
| Professional references | reference-response analysis |
| Meeting or interview | transcript/meeting analysis |
| High-risk technical stack | infrastructure, venue, custody, security, or operational research |

Do not run a routine without its minimum identifying or subject-matter inputs. Report it as blocked and name the missing input.
