# Council output shapes

## Direct answer

Lead with the recommendation, then evidence, main risks, dissent, and what would change the view.

## Comparison

Compare options against explicit criteria. Include strongest case for each, implementation friction, failure modes, and the council view.

## Decision memo

Include question, recommendation, rationale, counterarguments, risks and dependencies, dissent, unresolved evidence, and decision triggers.

## Full debate

Include the brief, selected seats, independent views, cross-examination, chair synthesis, dissent register, and human decision required.
