# Evaluation protocol

## Fixed cases

Include expected contract family, required topics, expected material facts, allowed source types, withheld evaluator reference, and forbidden cross-matter identifiers.

## Gates

- Classification meets the recorded threshold.
- Required topics and dependencies are covered.
- Every selected clause and material fact has attributable provenance.
- No invented party, identifier, date, price, regulatory scope, licence, signatory, or execution fact appears.
- Cross-matter leakage is zero tolerance.
- Blind replay keeps the historical reference outside generator inputs.
- Clean and review files differ only through intended review controls.
- Audit hashes and source maps reproduce.
- OOXML and rendered-page checks pass.

## Decision

Passing means the package may enter human legal review. It does not establish legal accuracy, template approval, signature readiness, or permission for external delivery. Record live-shadow status separately.
