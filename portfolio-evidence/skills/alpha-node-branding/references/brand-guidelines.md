# Alpha Node Brand Guidelines

## Positioning

Alpha Node should appear as a regulated, transparent, innovative financial brand that helps institutional and high-net-worth investors invest, stake, and store digital assets. The tone is confident, precise, professional, and future-facing.

Use language such as:

- regulated digital asset products
- transparent access to future finance
- institutional-grade investing, staking, and custody/storage
- invest with confidence in the future of finance

Avoid hype, memes, retail speculation, slang, and unsupported claims.

## Logo

Use only official assets from `assets/brand-assets/`. Preferred logo formats:

- SVG for web and scalable digital work.
- PNG for documents, slides, and raster layouts.
- EPS/AI for professional print and vector workflows.
- PSD/AI source files only when editing in design software.

Rules:

- Preserve clear space at least equal to the logo height on all sides.
- Do not slant, stretch, warp, distort, recreate, recolor, add transparency, or apply special effects.
- Always preserve the original logo aspect ratio. Never independently adjust logo width and height.
- Choose positive, negative, black, white, or all-white variants based on background contrast.

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary deep blue | Pantone 302 | `#004165` |
| Bright cyan | Pantone 298 | `#00B7E4` |
| Magenta accent | Pantone Magenta | `#D33982` |
| Dark slate | Pantone 296 | `#031E2F` |
| Neutral | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Use dark slate/deep blue as the foundation, cyan for emphasis and data accents, magenta sparingly for calls to action or highlights, and white for clarity.

## Typography

Primary brand typeface: Futura PT, used for titles and body text in the brand guide.

Bundled font files also include Gilmer Regular, Medium, and Heavy. Use Gilmer as a practical local fallback when Futura PT is unavailable or when a sharper logo-adjacent display style is useful.

For web fallbacks, use `Futura PT`, `Gilmer`, `Avenir Next`, `Montserrat`, `Arial`, `sans-serif`.

## Visual Style

Use sleek, modern, professional imagery with mono slate or dark-blue treatments. Graphic elements should support the composition with clean arrows, checks, gradients, or geometric accents. Keep layouts precise, uncluttered, and credible for financial services.

## Applications

Documents and PDFs:

- Use a strong cover with logo, title, date/version, and restrained brand graphics.
- Create clear section dividers using dark slate/deep blue.
- Keep tables and financial data dense but readable.

PowerPoint:

- Use dark title and section slides.
- Keep charts clean, with cyan and magenta used only for emphasis.
- Place logos consistently in headers, footers, or cover pages.

Web pages:

- Show the brand/product clearly in the first viewport.
- Use real brand assets, not generic abstract crypto decoration.
- Maintain strong contrast and institutional tone.

Social and banners:

- Use one message per asset.
- Keep logo readable at small sizes.
- Prefer high-contrast versions over low-contrast decorative placements.
