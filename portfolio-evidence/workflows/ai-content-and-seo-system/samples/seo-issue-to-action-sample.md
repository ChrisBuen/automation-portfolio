# SEO Issue-to-Action Presentation Sample

The source audit reviewed a content tracker containing roughly 23 live article records. It found a sound base structure alongside specific crawl and internal-link risks.

| Observed issue | Evidence at review time | Action defined | Outcome claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cornerstone content linked to a draft destination | Draft URL recorded in the source tracker | Replace with the live canonical destination | No post-fix ranking claim |
| Orphaned articles | 11 article records had no tracked incoming links | Add two or three relevant contextual links per article | No traffic claim |
| Missing trust and workflow hubs | Four planned hubs were referenced before publication | Prioritize hubs by number of dependent links | No indexing claim |
| Possible keyword overlap | Four topic pairs shared similar intent | Differentiate angle, title, intro, and cross-link framing | No cannibalization-resolution claim |
| Indexing uncertainty | Crawl permissions and `index, follow` were present | Verify coverage, sitemap, inspection, and manual-action state in Search Console | Diagnostic only |

## Editorial control

The audit also found production-note text that should not appear in a published article. The remediation was explicit: remove it from live copy and keep editorial instructions in the workspace instead.

This extract removes personal author names, draft IDs, private tracker paths, and article-specific URLs.
