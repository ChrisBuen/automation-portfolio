# Article package operations

Use a complete folder per article:

```text
articles/draft/<slug>/
  index.md
  research.json
  build_article_visuals.py
  source-assets/{first-party,official,licensed}/
  image-qc.md
```

Keep Markdown and research JSON canonical. Move the whole folder to `articles/posted/` only after CMS verification. Do not split the copy, source evidence, builder, and quality notes.

Frontmatter should carry title, slug, excerpt, category, primary and secondary keywords, search intent, audience, SEO title/description, focus keyphrase, related keyphrases, and featured-image reference.

Use an approved live-content inventory for internal links. Place external citations beside supported claims and prefer primary sources. Remove planning headings and raw link lists before publishing.
