# Editorial gates

Stop publication when any gate fails:

- a factual claim lacks a nearby credible source;
- an internal URL is not in the approved inventory;
- the exact focus phrase is absent from title and description where natural;
- three sentences repeat the same opening pattern;
- passive voice exceeds the chosen threshold;
- a paragraph exceeds 150 words or a section runs 300 words without a subheading;
- the article contains draft-only planning notes;
- a visual contains placeholder copy, unlicensed media, clipped text, or unreadable type;
- a named product is represented by a fabricated logo or lettermark;
- the visual builder still produces generic scaffolding instead of article-specific evidence;
- the CMS draft has not been checked after upload.

Treat a CMS SEO score as one diagnostic, not evidence of traffic or quality by itself.
