# Weekly market report runbook

Keep raw API responses beside readable extracts. Record fetch time, reporting window, URL, asset, units, incomplete periods, and capture limitations.

1. Establish the news set.
2. Capture the event calendar.
3. Fetch daily market data and raw candles.
4. Capture licensed analysis through a local credentialed adapter.
5. Fetch structured flows.
6. Generate charts from stored raw data.
7. Research and select the highest-signal stories.
8. Draft Markdown against the dated source pack.
9. Build DOCX from a reviewed template.
10. Validate the package, render every page, and inspect all charts.

Reconcile dates, link material claims, keep repeated asset sections structurally distinct, reject decorative visuals, and ensure every chart uses the dataset named in its source line. Keep credentials out of captures and reports.
