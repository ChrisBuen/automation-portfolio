# Public evidence policy

This portfolio publishes only evidence that can be reviewed without exposing credentials, client records, private contracts, regulated case material, staff contacts, or proprietary infrastructure.

Public examples use one of four labels: implemented/evaluated, runnable public proof, source extract, or design-stage/scaffold. Fictional data is identified inside the artifact. Aggregated private results are published only when the underlying claim can be traced internally and the aggregate reveals no sensitive content.

Production exports are rebuilt with fictional IDs, `example.invalid` addresses, and environment-variable configuration. Documents and images receive metadata, text, and visual checks before release.

The source projects remain unchanged. Private staging, hashes, and sanitization notes stay outside the public repository through `.gitignore`.
