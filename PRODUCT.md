# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

AI automation teams, prospective clients, hiring managers, and technical collaborators who receive the website through an application, resume, or direct introduction. They need to understand ChrisBuen's systems thinking, implementation ownership, control discipline, and supporting evidence without reading the full repository first.

## Product Purpose

Turn a large technical portfolio into a guided proof experience. Success means a visitor can explain what ChrisBuen builds, understand the recurring evidence-to-release operating model, distinguish the five flagship systems, inspect the five reusable methods, and follow public evidence into the complete repository.

## Brand Personality

Controlled, investigative, and kinetic. The tone is technically fluent and commercially understandable, with confidence coming from inspectable work rather than promotional claims.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI startup landing pages built from floating dashboards, neon grids, purple gradients, and ornamental glass cards.
- SaaS conversion templates with logo walls, fake metrics, testimonials, pricing, or repeated calls to action.
- n8n-themed developer portfolios that lead with tools instead of operational problems and accountable outcomes.
- Dense documentation portals that expose the archive without first establishing a clear narrative.
- Motion-heavy award-site experiments that delay reading, hijack scrolling, or collapse on mobile and reduced-motion settings.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the actual system.** Use the generated workflow maps and decision trees as the visual authority for intake, routing, validation, review, correction, and release. Motion only supports orientation.
2. **Evidence before promotion.** Every important claim must connect to public source material or an inspectable artifact.
3. **Complexity with orientation.** Reveal depth progressively while preserving a clear scan path through the page.
4. **Human control remains visible.** Automation must visibly pause, escalate, reconcile, or wait wherever accountability remains with a person.
5. **One authored world.** The five cases may change their working surface, but they share one material language, motion grammar, and point of view.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve semantic reading order, keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, touch-safe controls, useful alternative text, and complete reduced-motion and no-JavaScript experiences. Mobile layouts must retain the complete chart topology through intentional orientation and inspection controls rather than compressing it into a misleading linear list.
